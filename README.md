# Hi, I'm Krupa Nanda 👋

### QA Engineer · Long term WordPress Contributor

I'm a QA Engineer with 8+ years of experience testing and improving WordPress-based projects.

I'm passionate about **software quality, accessibility, open source, and creating better contributor experiences**.

Over the years, my WordPress contribution journey has grown from testing into release leadership, accessibility, mentoring, documentation, and community work.

## 🌱 What I contribute to

### 🧪 WordPress Testing & Quality

I contribute through exploratory testing, release testing, issue reporting, regression testing, and helping identify problems before they reach users.

### 🚀 WordPress Releases

I've served as **Test Lead for WordPress 6.8 and 6.9** and as a **Release Coordinator for WordPress 7.1**, working alongside contributors across teams to help major WordPress releases move forward with quality and confidence.

### ♿ Accessibility

I serve as a **WordPress Accessibility Team Representative**, contributing to accessibility testing, documentation, discussions, team coordination, and contributor support.

### 📚 Mentoring & Documentation

I enjoy helping contributors find their way into open source. I've mentored contributors through the **WordPress Credits Program** and contribute to documentation and contributor resources.

### 🌏 WordPress Community

My contribution extends beyond code and testing. I've supported Contributor Days and WordPress community events, including **WordCamp Asia**, **WordPress Accessibility Day**, and local WordCamps.
